# AudioGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

AudioGuard is published by Minh Tin Duong. This policy explains how the AudioGuard app for iPhone and Apple Watch handles your information.

**Summary.** AudioGuard does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share your personal information. All sound analysis happens on your device.

**Microphone.** When you turn on Microphone Capture, AudioGuard listens for safety alarms using a model that runs on your iPhone. The audio is analyzed as it is captured. It is not saved and is not sent anywhere.

**Audio files you choose.** If you select audio files in Test Audio, they are analyzed on your iPhone. AudioGuard does not copy them to a server or keep them after the analysis.

**Settings.** Your choices, such as language, appearance, detection rule, and alert switches, are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

**Notifications.** Alerts are created on your iPhone. AudioGuard does not use a server to send them.

**Apple Watch.** If you use the Watch app, your iPhone sends the alarm type, a confidence value, and your language choice directly to your paired Apple Watch.

**What AudioGuard does not do.** AudioGuard has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not use your location or contacts.

**Children.** AudioGuard is intended for workers and is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

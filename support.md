# AudioGuard Support

AudioGuard listens for construction and work-zone safety alarms and alerts you with a banner, a spoken warning, a notification, and Apple Watch haptics.

**Important.** AudioGuard is an assistive aid. It can miss alarms or raise false alerts, and it does not replace PPE, spotters, or site safety procedures. Stay alert to your surroundings at all times.

**Requirements.** iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later is optional.

**Languages.** English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish. Choose one in Settings.

## Common questions

- **The banner says Not Listening.** Turn on Microphone Capture on the main screen.
- **Microphone Capture turns itself off.** Open iOS Settings, find AudioGuard, and allow Microphone.
- **I do not get notifications.** Open iOS Settings, find AudioGuard, and allow Notifications.
- **Voice alerts are in English.** Download the voice for your language in iOS Settings under Accessibility, in the Voices section.
- **The Apple Watch does not vibrate.** Keep AudioGuard open on the Watch for the fastest haptics. With the iPhone locked, alerts can reach the Watch as notifications.
